Neptune TV is a native media player for Android TV/Google TV

(Does not work with Android Mobile on Phones/Tablets)

Compatible with Xtream, Stalker and M3U/M3U_Plus protocols.
Connect your server and enjoy an interface designed for users,
fast and smooth on Android TV boxes.

IMPORTANT: Neptune TV does not provide, host or sell any content.
You must have your own content to use the application.

MAIN FEATURES:
• Live TV Channels, Movies and TV Series with category navigation
• 3 display modes for Live TV Channels, including a mode with video preview
• 5 display modes for Movies and TV Series (1 list mode and 4 poster modes of different sizes)
• Programme guide (EPG) with current and upcoming programmes
• Category hiding for Live TV Channels, Movies and TV Series (via the remote or the Webif)
• Favourites management for TV channels (via the remote or the Webif)
• Zap History — view the list of already-watched channels and zap back without leaving Live TV
• Browse TV categories (or Favourites) in fullscreen and zap without leaving Live TV
• Replay/Catch-up TV up to 5 days back on compatible TV channels
• History with automatic resume playback for movies and TV series episodes
• Trailer for movies when available
• Audio track and subtitle management for Live TV Channels, Movies and TV Series
• Search (voice or manual entry) across all categories for movies and TV series
• "My List" lets you save movies or series to watch later
• NAS (SMB) management to view your own Audio/Photo/Video content (MP4, MKV, MP3, FLAC, etc.)
• Multi-Profiles: each with its own favourites, history and settings
• Interface in 10 languages: EN, FR, DE, ES, IT, PT, NL, PL, TR, AR
• Parental control via PIN code
• Choice between built-in player (recommended) or external player (Nova Player, VLC, etc.)
• Adjustable buffer to suit your connection quality
• Virtual remote control to drive the app from a PC or mobile
• Web interface (Webif) to create your Profiles, Favourites, etc. from a PC, and much more...
• Full backup and restore of your configuration via the Webif
• Various themes and wallpapers available in the Settings menu
• Customisable home screen wallpaper by uploading your own image via the Webif

Neptune TV is a COMPLETELY free application with no limitations and no recurring subscription.
An app made by a user, for users! The best for free!

PRIVACY:
No personal data is collected or transmitted to third-party servers.
Your login credentials and preferences remain stored only on your device.
